Dr. Amelia Hart

MD, FACC · Director of Preventive Cardiology

Dr. Hart believes the most powerful cardiology happens before a crisis. She has spent fourteen years helping people understand their risk, change the trajectory of their heart health and feel genuinely confident about the future.

Her clinic focuses on thorough assessment, clear explanation and practical plans that fit real lives. She works closely with imaging and intervention colleagues so every patient receives connected, continuous care.

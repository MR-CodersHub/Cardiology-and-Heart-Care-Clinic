Echocardiogram: a clear picture of how your heart works.

A painless ultrasound that shows your heart's structure, valves and pumping function in real time — giving your cardiologist the detail needed to make confident decisions.

About the test

See beyond the surface.

An echocardiogram uses sound waves to create moving images of your heart. It helps us assess chamber size, pumping strength, valve function and blood flow — without radiation, needles or discomfort.

Your specialist will explain each image as they capture it, so you leave understanding exactly what we found and what it means for you.

Pumping function

Measures ejection fraction and how well the heart muscle contracts.

Valve assessment

Detects narrowing, leakage and valve motion abnormalities.

Blood flow

Doppler imaging reveals the direction and speed of flow through the heart.

Early detection

Identifies subtle changes before symptoms appear.

What to expect

Your visit, step by step.

No surprises. Here is exactly how your appointment will unfold.

1
Arrive & settle

Check in, get comfortable and change into a gown in your private room.

2
Sensors placed

Small stickers are applied to your chest to record your heart rhythm.

3
Images captured

A painless probe glides over your chest while we capture live images.

4
Results explained

Your cardiologist walks you through the findings and next steps.

Preparation is minimal.

Wear comfortable clothing, avoid caffeine two hours before and take your usual medications unless told otherwise.

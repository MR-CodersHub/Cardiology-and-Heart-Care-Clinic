Privacy Policy

How Cardia Heart & Vascular Clinic handles the personal information you share with us.

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Cardia Heart & Vascular Clinic (“Cardia”, “we”, “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

This website is a demonstration. Forms on this site do not send, store or process personal data.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as your name, phone number and email address.
  • Appointment preferences, including the service, doctor, date and consultation type you select.
  • Messages you send through our enquiry forms or by email.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the website reliable and secure.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and arrange or manage appointments.
  • To provide clinical services, follow-up care and appointment reminders.
  • To improve our website, services and patient experience.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and professional record-keeping obligations.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with clinicians and care teams involved in your treatment, trusted service providers who support our operations, or authorities when required by law. Any sharing is limited to what is necessary and subject to appropriate safeguards.

Data security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to legal and clinical record-keeping requirements. You may also ask us to stop sending non-essential communications at any time.

Cookies and analytics

This demonstration site may use browser storage for preferences such as light or dark mode and text direction. A production version of this site would describe any analytics or marketing cookies here and offer appropriate controls.

Children’s privacy

Our website is intended for adults and is not directed at children. Where a child receives care, information is provided by a parent or legal guardian and handled in line with applicable requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the date of the most recent revision shown above.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at care@cardiaclinic.com or call +1 (800) 555-0192.

Our story

Built on a simple belief: heart care should feel human.

Cardia began in 2009 with one consultation room and a conviction that patients deserve time, clarity and compassion alongside clinical excellence.

Dr. Jonathan Reyes · Founder
Where it began

A clinic created because patients deserved better.

Our founder saw too many people leave appointments confused, anxious and unsure of their next step. He imagined something different — a practice where the medicine is rigorous and the experience is reassuring.

Seventeen years later, that idea guides everything we do. We take the time to listen, explain and involve you in every decision. Our specialists work as one team, so you never have to repeat your story or chase your own records.

“The best cardiology is the kind that helps people stop being patients and start living again.”

What guides us

Our values, in everyday practice.

Not words on a wall — the standards our team is held to in every interaction.

Compassion first

We treat the person, not just the rhythm. Every question is welcome and no concern is too small.

Uncompromising precision

Evidence-led protocols, accredited facilities and continuous clinical review keep our standards high.

Team-based care

Cardiologists, imaging specialists and nurses coordinate your care so nothing falls through the cracks.

Clarity always

We explain findings in plain language and give you written summaries you can revisit at home.

Prevention mindset

Our greatest success is helping people avoid heart disease before it starts.

Respect for time

Punctual appointments, same-day reporting and digital access to everything you need.

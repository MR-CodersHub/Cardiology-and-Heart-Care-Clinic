Diagnostics

Advanced testing, explained simply.

Select a test to learn what it involves, how long it takes and when results arrive.

Electrocardiogram (ECG)

A quick, painless recording of your heart's electrical activity. It can reveal irregular rhythms, previous heart attacks and conduction problems.

Duration: 10 minutes
Results: Same day
Preparation: None
Where: Diagnostics lab

Echocardiogram

An ultrasound of your heart that shows how the chambers and valves work. We use it to assess function, valve disease and congenital conditions.

Duration: 45 minutes
Results: Within 24 hours
Preparation: None
Where: Imaging suite

Stress Test

Monitors how your heart responds to exercise or medication, helping detect coronary artery disease and guide treatment.

Duration: 60 minutes
Results: 1–2 days
Preparation: Light meal beforehand
Where: Stress lab

Holter Monitoring

A wearable recorder tracks your heart rhythm over 24–48 hours while you go about normal life, catching intermittent problems.

Duration: 24–48 hours
Results: 2–3 days
Preparation: Wear comfortable clothing
Where: Fitted on site

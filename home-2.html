Treatment pathways

Care that meets you where you are.

Explore how we diagnose, treat and prevent heart conditions.

Stay ahead of heart disease.

Our preventive programme maps your personal risk using blood pressure, lipid profile, family history and lifestyle, then builds a plan to lower it.

  • Comprehensive cardiovascular risk assessment
  • Cholesterol, glucose and inflammatory marker testing
  • Nutrition and movement planning with our specialists

Clarity, quickly.

On-site ECG, echocardiography, Holter monitoring and stress testing with specialist reporting ” usually within the same day.

  • Same-day appointments and reporting
  • Private, purpose-built imaging suites
  • Digital reports in your patient portal

Evidence-led, individually tailored.

From medication optimisation to interventional procedures, your cardiologist explains every option and helps you choose with confidence.

  • Medication management with clear schedules
  • Minimally invasive interventional procedures
  • Second opinions from specialist consultants

Support that continues after you leave.

Structured follow-ups, cardiac rehabilitation guidance and reminders mean you are never left wondering what to do next.

  • Personalised recovery roadmap
  • Medication and appointment reminders
  • Secure video check-ins
